# Research brief

Write the brief to `work/<taskId>/brief.md` if you can write files; otherwise put a
compressed version in your result summary.

1. **Answer first**: 2-3 sentences that answer the goal.
2. **Evidence**: bullet per claim, each with its source.
3. **Disagreements**: where sources conflict and which you trust more, and why.
4. **Unknowns**: what you could not establish.
5. **Sources**: list of URLs/paths.
